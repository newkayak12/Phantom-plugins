Reports HTTP header names in Spring code that cannot be valid or that are one typo away from a standard header, such as Content_Type, Authorisation or Acept.

Checked: @RequestHeader, the headers of request mappings, @CrossOrigin allowed and exposed headers, and the header methods of HttpHeaders, RestClient, WebClient, ResponseEntity, MockMvc and the servlet request and response.

Custom headers (X-Tenant-Id) and placeholders are not reported.
