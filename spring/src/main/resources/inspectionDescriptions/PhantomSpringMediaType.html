Reports media types in Spring code that do not parse as type/subtype, use a top-level type that does not exist, or are one typo away from a common type, such as aplication/json or image/jpg.

Checked: produces and consumes of request mappings, MediaType.parseMediaType and valueOf, contentType(...) and accept(...) of the HTTP clients and MockMvc, and Content-Type/Accept header values. Vendor types such as application/vnd.acme+json are fine.
