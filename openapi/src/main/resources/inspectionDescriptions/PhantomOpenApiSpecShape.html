Reports YAML and JSON files that look like an OpenAPI or Swagger document but are not read as one, and problems at the top level of documents that are:
  • paths and info at the top with a misspelled version key (opeanpi:, swager:) or none at all;
  • a version no tool reads: openapi must be 3.x or 3.x.y, swagger must be 2.0;
  • top-level keys that are not fields of the document (weak warning); extensions starting with x- are fine.
