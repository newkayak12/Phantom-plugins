Reports a cron expression that Spring or Quartz would reject at runtime: a wrong number of fields, a value outside its field's range, a malformed range, step, L, W or #, and for Quartz a missing or doubled ? in day-of-month and day-of-week.

Checked: @Scheduled(cron = ...), CronExpression.parse and new CronTrigger (Spring), CronScheduleBuilder.cronSchedule, new CronExpression and CronTriggerFactoryBean.setCronExpression (Quartz). Only string literals; placeholders are skipped.
