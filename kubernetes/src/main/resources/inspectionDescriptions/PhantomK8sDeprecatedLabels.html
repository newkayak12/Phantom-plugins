Reports well-known labels, annotations and taint keys that Kubernetes renamed or no longer reads, such as beta.kubernetes.io/os (use kubernetes.io/os), failure-domain.beta.kubernetes.io/zone (use topology.kubernetes.io/zone), node-role.kubernetes.io/master, the kubernetes.io/ingress.class annotation and the alpha seccomp and AppArmor annotations. They are checked as keys of labels, annotations and nodeSelector, and as the key or topologyKey of affinity terms, tolerations and topology spread constraints.
