Reports keys of built-in resources (Pod, Deployment, StatefulSet, DaemonSet, ReplicaSet, Job, CronJob, Service, Ingress, ConfigMap, Secret, RBAC) that the API does not define, at the levels Phantom lists completely: the top level, metadata, workload and Service spec, the pod template, pod spec, containers and container ports. Also reports missing metadata.name, spec.selector/spec.template of workloads, schedule/jobTemplate of CronJobs and container name/image. Files with template syntax ({{), such as Helm templates, are skipped.
