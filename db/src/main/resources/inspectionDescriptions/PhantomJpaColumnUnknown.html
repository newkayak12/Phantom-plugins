Reports an entity attribute whose column is not in the entity's table in the database of the project's data source.

Column names follow @Column(name), @JoinColumn(name) or the attribute name, and the naming strategy. Attributes of embeddables count (with @AttributeOverride), and so do those of mapped superclasses; a missing one of those is reported on the entity. A @ManyToOne without @JoinColumn uses attribute_referencedColumn. Collections, @Transient, mappedBy sides, join tables and columns of secondary tables are not checked, nor entities with property access.
